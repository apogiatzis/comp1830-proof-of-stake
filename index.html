About this model

Each slot, one validator is chosen to propose a block, with probability proportional to its stake. Every online validator attests (votes) for the chain. At the end of each epoch, if validators holding at least ⅔ of the stake attested, the epoch is justified; two justified epochs in a row finalise the earlier one, which can then no longer be reverted.

It is a simplified teaching model. In Ethereum:

  • An epoch has 32 slots of 12 seconds (here: 8 slots).
  • Proposers are chosen pseudo-randomly (RANDAO), weighted by effective balance; most rewards come from attesting rather than proposing.
  • Each “validator” here stands for a staking entity, which in reality would run many validators of 32–2048 ETH each.
  • Slashing applies a small initial penalty plus a correlation penalty that grows with the total stake slashed in the same period (here: 3 × slashed share, applied immediately).
  • The inactivity leak starts after 4 epochs without finality and drains offline validators until the online ones hold ⅔ again (here: a flat 5 % per epoch).
